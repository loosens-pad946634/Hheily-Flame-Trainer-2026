## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sucker for Love: Crush Landing**. This trainer helps manage the game's timed apartment scenarios and Hheily's fading flame. It can pause countdown pressure, restore flame strength, protect useful objects from being consumed, and make color-based feeding puzzles easier to manage.

**Target Audience:** Useful for players who want to explore every apartment interaction, test alternate choices, solve color-based feeding puzzles without resource pressure, or replay the story while reducing timed-event difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Event Timer** — Pauses the active countdown during timed story events, giving you more time to inspect the apartment and plan your next action.
- **Restore Hheily's Flame** — Restores the comet's flame strength when it begins to fade.
- **Infinite Flame Stability** — Prevents Hheily's flame from losing strength during extended puzzle and exploration sequences.
- **Preserve Consumed Objects** — Keeps apartment objects available after feeding them to Hheily, allowing you to experiment with different item and color combinations.
- **Color Puzzle Assistance** — Helps manage the color requirements used to strengthen Hheily and reduces the risk of exhausting useful color-matched objects.
- **Door Event Protection** — Prevents time pressure from increasing while handling visitors and conversations at the apartment door.
- **Choice Recovery** — Provides a safer way to test important dialogue and interaction choices without immediately losing progress.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Failure Prevention** — Protects against flame depletion and other scenario failures during dangerous interactions. Crush Landing has no conventional combat system.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the apartment by examining interactive objects and moving between available areas. The trainer does not replace normal navigation; use the timer controls when you need extra time to inspect rooms, read clues, or decide which objects to use.

### Tools & Inventory

Crush Landing revolves around collecting and selecting objects found around the apartment, including items from counters, the refrigerator, and the freezer. Preserve Consumed Objects is recommended when testing different offerings, since used items may no longer be available during the current scenario.

### World & Environment

The apartment is the central setting, with visitors arriving at the door and additional interactions available through conversations, phone calls, letters, and the hallway. Freeze Event Timer and Door Event Protection are best used before starting extended conversations or investigating optional events.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no traditional combat system with weapons, enemies, or combat encounters. Most danger comes from incorrect choices, depleted flame strength, timed events, and unsettling story interactions. Use Restore Hheily's Flame or Failure Prevention when experimenting with risky actions.
